DatePreImage
A day decodes to its UTC midnight: an instant off midnight equals no day.
DecimalPreImage
A DECIMAL decodes to a float, exactly invertible up to precision 15 only. A byte decimal's edges are 8 big-endian bytes: the Decimal order sign-extends any width.
DoublePreImage
Float32PreImage
A FLOAT decodes to the exact double of its 32 bits: a literal between two of them equals neither, and one beyond the largest float32 lies between it and the infinity.
IntegerPreImage
A float literal compares exactly up to 2^53: a fraction lies between two integers and equals neither.
TimestampPreImage
Millis hit a micros literal only on the milli grid; nanos decode floored to micros.
UnsignedInt64PreImage
UINT_64 decodes to a digit string; its physical value is the INT64 bit pattern, ordered unsigned. A float compares numerically, a fraction lying between two integers.
UuidPreImage
Only the canonical lowercase form: the column decodes to it, so another spelling never equals a value.
